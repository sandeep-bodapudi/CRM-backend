# CRM: changes that would save time (2026-11-02)

These suggestions come from working through the code, the audits and today's releases. They are ordered by
**time saved for the least effort**. None of them is built yet. Each one says who it helps and roughly how
big it is (S = a day or less, M = 2–4 days, L = a week or more).

## Quick wins

| #   | Change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Who it helps         | Why it saves time                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Size      |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1   | **Keep the API awake.** Move api-rscrm to Render's always-on plan (about $7 a month).                                                                                                                                                                                   | Everyone             | The free plan sleeps after 15 minutes, so the first person each morning, and anyone after a quiet spell, waits 30–60 s for the app to load. Night jobs (reminders, absence marking, the last-day lock-out) only run when someone wakes the server. | S (owner) |
| 2   | **Pre-fill the daily report from the CRM record.** Today's work page now records every action, so the report can open with "You logged 42 calls, moved 6 leads to Qualified, booked 2 visits" already filled in. The person only adds a short note.                     | Everyone who reports | Writing the report is a daily 10–15 minute job of re-typing what the CRM already knows. It is also where the numbers get padded.                                                                                                                   | S         |
| 3   | **One-tap outcomes after a call.** When a telecaller comes back to the app after a call, show buttons such as "Interested / Call back tomorrow / Not reachable / Wrong number / Site visit" that log the call, set the next call-back and change the status in one tap. | Telecallers          | Each call today means opening the lead, logging the call, then setting a task and a status separately: 4–6 taps and typing, 100+ times a day.                                                                                                      | M         |
| 4   | **Approve from the notification.** Leave, early logout, bookings, payments, project approval and site-visit acceptance get "Approve / Reject" buttons right on the push notification and in the Approvals inbox list, without opening each record.                      | MD, HR, PMs, Finance | Approvals are the main reason managers open the app. Most are a yes.                                                                                                                                                                               | M         |
| 5   | **Hide the "System Default Actor" account** from employee lists and dropdowns.                                                                                                                                                                                          | HR, managers         | It shows up in the Employees directory and the task assignee list (seen today) and can be picked by mistake.                                                                                                                                       | S         |
| 6   | **Fill in the 10 missing reporting managers.** The HR banner already offers "Review & apply".                                                                                                                                                                           | Managers             | Without a manager, "My team" views and team approvals miss those people.                                                                                                                                                                           | S (HR)    |

## Bigger wins

| #   | Change                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Who it helps       | Why it saves time                                                                                       | Size |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- |
| 7   | **Customer WhatsApp reminders for site visits:** the day before, and 2 hours before, with the location pin and the PM's number.                                                                               | PMs, telecallers   | No-shows waste a PM's half day; today the reminder call is made by hand.                                | M    |
| 8   | **Shareable cost sheet.** From a unit, one tap makes a PDF or WhatsApp message with the price breakdown (the pricing engine already calculates it), project photos and the payment plan.                      | Sales, telecallers | Sales staff currently build quotes by hand or by screenshot. This also stops wrong prices being quoted. | M    |
| 9   | **Bulk actions on the lead list:** select many leads, then reassign, change status, add a tag, or send a WhatsApp template.                                                                                   | Sales managers, MD | Moving or cleaning leads is one at a time today.                                                        | M    |
| 10  | **"My queue" first screen for calling staff:** overdue call-backs, then today's call-backs, then fresh leads, in that order, with one "Next lead" button.                                                     | Telecallers        | Less time deciding whom to call next, and fewer forgotten call-backs.                                   | M    |
| 11  | **Automatic payment reminders and receipts:** an instalment due in 3 days sends the customer a WhatsApp or SMS; a payment marked SUCCESS sends a PDF receipt; Finance sees one list of what is due this week. | Finance            | Collections follow-up is manual today.                                                                  | M–L  |
| 12  | **A weekly summary for the MD** every Monday (e-mail or WhatsApp): leads, visits, bookings, collections, who was absent, top and bottom performers, and anything waiting on them.                             | MD                 | It replaces opening four dashboards to answer "how did last week go?".                                  | S–M  |
| 13  | **Field staff work offline.** Visit photos, check-in proofs and notes are saved on the phone when there is no signal at a site and sent when it returns.                                                      | PMs, agents        | Site locations often have weak signal; failed uploads are redone.                                       | L    |

## Keeping the system healthy (saves firefighting time)

| #   | Change                                                                                                                                                  | Why                                                                                                                                                                | Size                 |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| 14  | **Fix the ~150 old failing automated tests**, then make CI block on them.                                                                               | Today a real break can hide among old failures, so every release needs a long manual check.                                                                        | L                    |
| 15  | **Error tracking (Sentry or similar).**                                                                                                                 | Errors are only in Render logs, which nobody reads. The Technical Manager dashboard shows job failures, but not app crashes.                                       | S (needs an account) |
| 16  | **Archive the activity record.** Move CRM_ACTION rows older than 6 months into a monthly summary table.                                                 | The new daily-work record adds one row per change, roughly 2–5 thousand a day at the current team size. That is fine for a year, but should be trimmed after that. | S                    |
| 17  | **Put the two public websites on GitHub** (with the password-like strings removed from their history) and give them the same release script as the CRM. | Website updates are a manual build and upload today.                                                                                                               | S                    |

## Suggested order

1, 5, 6 (owner/HR, same day) → 2 → 3 → 4 → 10 → 7 → 8 → 12, with 14 and 15 alongside.
